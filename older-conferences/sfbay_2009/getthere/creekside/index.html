Venue

Creekside Inn

Erlang Training and Consulting and the speakers will be staying at the Creekside Inn which is a 20 minute walk from the Sheraton. There is also a shuttle service that will take guests to and from the conference. Should delegates wish to stay here, Erlang Training and Consulting have negotiated a rate of $149.00 excl. taxes. This rate includes:

  • Local shuttle service with a brand new Cadillac or mini van
  • Pastries and coffee each morning
  • Local and toll-free phone calls
  • Room delivery of the Wall Street Journal
  • Lobby computer with printer, DSL email and WiFi connection
  • Heated outdoor swimming pool
  • Spacious Fitness Room with brand new equipment
  • Bottled water
  • In-room and meeting room high speed internet
