Erlang Solutions Ltd

Erlang Solutions Ltd. is an international company that specialises in the open source language Erlang and its middleware OTP. It was founded soon after Erlang was released as open source in 1999, with an aim to fill a vacuum similar to the one that existed before companies such as SUSE and Red Hat came in to support Linux.With offices in the UK, Sweden and Poland, combined with the most experienced Erlang developers in the industry, we serve clients worldwide. We employ a network of certified Erlang/OTP consultants, provide services to private individuals and blue chip companies alike and cooperate with universities in different research projects. We contribute a range of Erlang products to the open-source community, including the Erlang Web, we organise the Erlang Factory conferences in London and The San Francisco Bay Area, we provide additional community services through the TrapExit community website and we organise the London Erlang User Group Meetings and Erlounges.  We also annually sponsor Bachelor, Master's and PhD theses, presenting the results at international conferences worldwide and every summer invite students to undergo internships in the company to get familiar with the Erlang language and gain experience.
