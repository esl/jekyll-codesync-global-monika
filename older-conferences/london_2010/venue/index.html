Conference Venue

The Erlang Factory will be hosted at The Old Sessions House, the heart of the city of London. This is the same fantastic venue that hosted last year’s Erlang eXchange and is accessible by rail, underground and road.

The Old Sessions House
22 Clerkenwell Green
London EC1R 0NA
United Kingdom


Getting There
Nearest tube:
Farringdon Station and Kings Cross Station are both near the venue

Public transport
Transport for London's Journey Planner provides up to the minute travel options on how to reach The Old Sessions House quickly and accurately.
Alternatively, you can call London Travel Information on 020 7222 1234 or visit the website.

Underground
The nearest Underground stations are Farringdon Station and Kings Cross Station, on the Circle, Metropolitan and Hammersmith & City lines.

Train
The nearest rail stations are Kings Cross, Farringdon and Blackfriars. City Thameslink services serve Barbican, Moorgate and Cannon Street.

Bus
Bus Route 55 stops on Clerkenwell Road, very near The Old Sessions House.

Hotels and Accomodation

The Erlang Factory is pleased to have secured GREAT CONFERENCE RATES at the City Hotel in London.

The hotel is located on the Osborn Street, very close to the Erlang Solutions office where all the training courses will be held and a short tube ride (5 minutes) to the conference venue. Speakers will be staying there alongside Erlang Solutions' employees from the Polish and Swedish offices.

For single or double room for sole use, delegates will pay only £89.00 per night; for double/twin room for 2 people – £109 and for double room with 1 double and 1 single bed for 3 people - £129.00. These prices include VAT, Buffer Breakfast and the Internet connection.

Book your hotel room NOW!
You can book for the special Erlang Factory rate direct with the City Hotel by contacting Tanya Vodoryez or Georgiana (reservation manager) directly on 020 7247 33 13(option 2) or by e-mail info@cityhotellondon.co.uk. Please mention "Erlang Factory" when booking a room.

If you do not want to stay at the City Hotel, there are many places in London to stay and you will be able to find something to suit every budget.
