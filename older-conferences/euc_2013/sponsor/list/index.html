Platinum Sponsors

Basho Technologies

Basho Technologies, Inc.,founded in January 2008 by a core group of Software Architects, Engineers and Executive Leadership from Akamai Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ:AKAM) is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts.  Basho produces Riak, a distributed datastore that combines high availability, easily-scalable capacity and throughput, and ease of use. Riak's complete availability means that applications built using Riak remain both read- and write-available under almost any operational conditions and without requiring intervention.  Available in both an open source and a paid commercial version, Riak provides unprecedented read- and write-availability to web, mobile, and enterprise applications.

Campanja

Campanja is creating a more intelligent way for companies to purchase online advertising, and we need the best talent to achieve this. We believe there is tremendous value in software that makes intelligent business decisions in real-time.

We have built several products using Erlang that have succeeded in improving our customers businesses, but we know that this is only the beginning.We believe Erlang is the solution to many of the challenges we are facing due to it's ability to hotswap code and handle big volumes of clicks while sustaining low latencies through it's natural concurrency.

We need people with the skills, energy, and creativity to build the bridge between business intelligence and online marketing. If you are up for a challenge, please stop by our booth during the conference. We will tell you all about our Stockholm-based office, the team, and how you can help us.

Klarna

Klarna was founded in 2005 and has since then become Europe's leading provider of online payment solutions. To date, millions of people have used our services through our more than 14 000 e-stores. We believe that the foundation in a company is its system, that's why we work with our own specially developed system that allows tailored solutions, and at the same time our experience in the industry creates a simple basis for business implementations.
