About Bruce

Bruce is the community manager at Industrial Erlang User Group. He is located with the Erlang/OTP team at Ericsson, providing both technical and community-based support, as well as managing the community site Erlang Central. The goal of the Industrial Erlang User Group is to represent the interest of the Erlang users towards Ericsson, to complement the work Ericsson is doing and help spread the awareness and usage of Erlang to secure its future health, well-being and commercial use.
