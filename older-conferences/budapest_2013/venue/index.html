Mike Williams, Robert Virding and Joe Armstrong will give a keynote talk at the Erlang User Conference

Having the inventors of Erlang Mike Williams, Robert Virding and Joe Armstrong all together on the same stage is a very rare occurrence. And when their talk is about their vast experience and how it influenced the developing of Erlang, you know '183 years of programming' is one keynote you do not want to miss.

The Early Bird admission will open on the 3rd of April, so be prepared to grab your ticket when Registration opens.
