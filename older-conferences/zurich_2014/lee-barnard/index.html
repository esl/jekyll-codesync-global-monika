The Evolution of Riak

The distributed systems company Basho Technologies has helped thousands of businesses scale and achieve incredible uptime’s by using their distributed database, Riak. Basho has introduced some new features into Riak, making it the most powerful K/V store on the market. Riak 2.0 brings enterprise Solr search integration, Data Types (CRDTs), Security features and, now the ability to have certain subsets of your data strongly consistent. This talk will take a look at some of these features.
