ErLounge - Thursday 21 March

The ErLounge party on the 21st of March is a chance to socialise over drinks and nibbles with speakers, conference attendants and other people who share your interest in Erlang. 

But wait, there's more! the Erlounge Lightning talks! 

Submit an entry for a 10-minute talk on a topic of your choice (please no tiramisu recipes, though :) and get your change to glory and the undying respect of your fellow ErLoungers. Submit your talk here.

Register for the Erlounge here if you are not registered for the Conference.

Erlang User Conference: Call for Talks open until 10 March

The conference will take place on 9-10 June. It will be followed by one day of tutorials on 11 June and 3 days of expert training on 11-13 June. 

We are currently accepting talk submissions for the Erlang User Conference 2014. If you have an interesting project you are working on or would like to share your knowledge, please submit your talk here. The deadline is 10th of March.
