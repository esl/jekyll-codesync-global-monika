Venue

The 2011 Erlang Factory will be held at the Hilton San Francisco Airport.

We are pleased to announce that we have managed to get really good prices for rooms at the Hilton for delegates and speakers. The room rate will be only $119.00 per night (including Internet and parking)with a choice of either a King size bed or two double beds, this is a large discount over the normal rate and that of equivalent hotels. Book your room at the dedicated Hilton reservation page for the Erlang Factory at this discounted rate by the 25 February. Don’t miss out on this fantastic saving!





Address
600 Airport Boulevard, Burlingame, CA 94010.



Getting there
The Hilton is situated close to San Francisco International Airport.

  • The hotel has 24-hour complimentary shuttle service to and from the San Francisco International Airport and hotel which Shuttle runs every 20 minutes.  Delegates will need to go to the departure level, walk to the centre island at the airport and locate the area for the hotel courtesy shuttle bus pick ups.  These areas are clearly marked and have courtesy phones.  Refer to the number for our Hotel, dial that number using the courtesy phone and notify us what area you are or what airline.  For those arriving on BART, they would actually be at the International Terminal.  They will again just need to go to the Hotel Shuttle Bus pick up area of the International Area, pick up the courtesy phone and call the Hotel.
  • The city of Burlingame has a trolley service that will pick guests up from the Caltrain Station and bring them to the hotels here in Burlingame, but it is a service that is not available on a 24-hour basis nor as frequent as the hotel Airport Shuttle. Please note that on the Trolley-service website map, the hotel on Airport Blvd marked "Sheraton" is now the San Francisco Airport Hilton.
  • The BART (Bay area Rapid Transport system) goes to the airport and delegates can connect with the Caltrain at Milbrae Station.
  • You can also arrive by car. See here for driving directions.


Parking
Self-parking is available at the hotel for day delegates at a special discounted rate of $5 per day. 

Alternative accommodation options
Should you wish to stay elsewhere, there are also many other hotels in the San Francisco Airport Area including areas such as Burlingame, Milbrae and San Bruno. A selection can be found here.
