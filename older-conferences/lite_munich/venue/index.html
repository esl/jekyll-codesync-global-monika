Venue

TNG Technology Consulting GmbH
Betastraße 13a
85774 Unterföhring
Germany

  • By rail: take S-Bahn line S8, exit at the stop Unterföhring, leave the station heading southwards, after 200 m turn left (to the East) towards the Heinrich-Hildebrand footpath, follow it for about 800 m
  • By bus: take line 233, exit at stop Betastraße
  • By car: see map; TNG-parking spaces are located directly in front of the main entry and in the underground car park. Further parking spaces can be found in the public underground parking "Park Village" located about 400 m northwards in the Betastraße on the left side.
