Venue

AF83
15 rue Poissonière
75002 Paris

af83 premises are located at the heart of the “Sentier” district, which concentrate the finest innovative tech and IT companies of the city. It’s always a welcoming place for all cutting-edge technologies and Open Source advocates.

af83 is near to the following subway stations: “Sentier”, on line 3, and “Bonne Nouvelle”, line  and 9. It's also 10min on foot from "Châtelet - Les Halles", which is the big central hub for public transport in Paris.
