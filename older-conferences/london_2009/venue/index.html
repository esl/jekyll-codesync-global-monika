Conference Venue

The Erlang Factory will be hosted at The Old Sessions House, the heart of the city of London. This is the same fantastic venue that hosted last year’s Erlang eXchange and is accessible by rail, underground and road.

The Old Sessions House
22 Clerkenwell Green
London EC1R 0NA
United Kingdom


Getting There
Nearest tube:
Farringdon Station and Kings Cross Station are both near the venue

Public transport
Transport for London's Journey Planner provides up to the minute travel options on how to reach The Old Sessions House quickly and accurately.
Alternatively, you can call London Travel Information on 020 7222 1234 or visit the website.

Underground
The nearest Underground stations are Farringdon Station and Kings Cross Station, on the Circle, Metropolitan and Hammersmith & City lines.

Train
The nearest rail stations are Kings Cross, Farringdon and Blackfriars. City Thameslink services serve Barbican, Moorgate and Cannon Street.

Bus
Bus Route 55 stops on Clerkenwell Road, very near The Old Sessions House.


Erlounge Venue

The Erlounge for the Erlang Factory will be held in the crypt of St. James Church, Clerkenwell:

The Crypt on the Green St James Clerkenwell Clerkenwell Close London EC1R 0EA

This is situated one block from the Sessions House. See here for directions.

Erlang Takes Over the World! New Erlang Factory Lites in Chicago, New York, Berlin and Tel Aviv

If you didn't get a chance to come to EUC or you did and now you want more Erlang in your life, fear not! The Erlang Factories Lite will be back in September. The new dates and places are New York - 14 September, Chicago - 4 October, Berlin -16 October, Tel Aviv -23 October.

The Erlang Factory Lite Chicago on 4 October will give you the chance to to meet Alexander Gounares, Adam Denenberg, Garrett Smith and Mahesh Paolini-Subramanya. The Very Early Bird prices are up, so take advantage while you still can.

By popular demand we're coming to Berlin on the 16th of October. Steve Vinoski already confirmed he will be one of our speakers, so hurry up and submit your talk.  

The first Erlang Factory in Israel will take place at the Google Campus in Tel Aviv on 23 October. Talks will be delivered both in English and in Hebrew. Talk submission is now open here.

The Erlang Factory Lite New York will soon be live on the website, so keep an eye on this space!
