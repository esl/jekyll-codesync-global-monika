18:00 - 19:30 Ten Minute Talks

Anyone can sign up and give a ten minute talk. Introduce your user group, an open source project, an Erlang project or just share ideas with other conference delegates. The ten minute talks session is open to all, not just the conference delegates and will be held in the Crypt opposite the conference venue. The talks will be followed by an Erlounge till late.
