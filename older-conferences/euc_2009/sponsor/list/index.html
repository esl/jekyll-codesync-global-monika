Gold Sponsors

Erlang Training and Consulting
Erlang Training and Consulting was founded in 1999 in London to provide Training, Consulting, Contracting and System Development services in Erlang and OTP. Today, With offices in London, Uppsala and Krakow we serve clients on five continents. We employ a network of certified Erlang/OTP consultants, provide services to private individuals and blue chip companies alike and cooperate with universities in different research projects. We contribute a range of Erlang products to the open-source community, including the Erlang Web, we organise the Erlang Factory conferences in London and The San Francisco Bay Area, we provide additional community services through the TrapExit community website and we organise the London Erlang User Group Meetings and Erlounges.  We also annually sponsor Bachelor, Master's and PhD theses, presenting the results at international conferences worldwide and every summer invite students to undergo internships in the company to get familiar with the Erlang language and gain experience. 

Ericsson
Ericsson is a world-leading provider of telecommunications equipment and related services to mobile and fixed network operators globally. Over 1,000 networks in more than 175 countries utilize our network equipment and 40 percent of all mobile calls are made through our systems. We are one of the few companies worldwide that can offer end-to-end solutions for all major mobile communication standards.

Through our Sony Ericsson Mobile Communications joint venture we offer a range of mobile devices, including those supporting multimedia applications and other services allowing richer communication.

We invest heavily in R&D and actively promote open standards and systems. Also reflecting our ongoing commitment to technological leadership, we have one of the industry's most comprehensive intellectual property portfolio containing over 23,000 patents.

Mobile Arts
Mobile Arts provides mobile messaging, advertisement and location solutions to mobile operators.

The company is renowned for its ability to provide carrier-grade platforms with truly innovative applications, which it can customize in short timescales to customer requirements. The customer base includes the full range of mobile operators, from small virtual operators to leading large operators from the MTS, Telenor and TeliaSonera groups. These operators provide Mobile Arts services to over 100 million end-users across 10 time zones.

Mobile Arts is headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden with offices in the UK, USA, Russia and Croatia.

Synapse Mobile Networks
Synapse Mobile Networks AB provides cost effective world class revenue generating products for 3GSM operators. Synapse is the inventor of and world leader in Automatic Device Management. Automatic Device Management is the most effective way to deliver advanced mobile services to the end-user. Our vision is to make a small enhancement to all peoples daily lives globally through user friendly communication services enabled by innovation and excellence in products and service.
