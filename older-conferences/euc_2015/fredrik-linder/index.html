Realtime performance at scale: the search for the Holy Grail

Fredrik Linder
Erlang Lead at Machine Zone


Realtime performance at scale: the search for the Holy Grail

Fredrik will go through some of the low level optimizations we had to do in Erlang/NIF at Machine Zone in order to reach extreme performance with close to zero contention to achieve millions of messages per second.

Talk objectives:

Share findings developing a massively distributed Erlang system at hyper-scale.

Target audience:

Erlang devs

About Fredrik

Fredrik has been working with Erlang and C++ most of his life and has a great passion for large scale distributed systems. He spent many years at Ericsson and Cellpoint as well as making a shorter stop at Klarna. Fredrik is now the Erlang Lead at Machine Zone in the US, working on various real time at large scale projects
